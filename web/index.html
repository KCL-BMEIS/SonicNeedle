Why make needles smart?

  • Finding the tip is hard. Needles are used for biopsies, injections and nerve blocks, but inside the body it's hard to see exactly where the tip is.
  • Ultrasound on a fibre. Using optical fibres thinner than a millimetre, we can make ultrasound transmitters and sensors that fit inside a needle.
  • Instruments that can see. Built into needles and other surgical tools, they make instruments smart: they can be tracked, navigated to a target and even image the tissue around them.

Our needle uses a bigger, off-the-shelf sensor, but the idea is the same: timing the echo tells you how far away the target is.
